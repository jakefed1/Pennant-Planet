# Pennant Planet

A nostalgic arcade baseball game from JDF Productions. Play an Exhibition game, draft a team for the Pennant Cup or a Season, or enter the Home Run Derby.

## Play in your browser

Once the site is live, [play Pennant Planet](https://jakefed1.github.io/Pennant-Planet/) in your browser. No download or installation is needed.

## Download and play offline

1. In this repository, click [`index.html`](index.html), then click **Download raw file**.
2. Open the downloaded `index.html` in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari. If it opens in a text editor, right-click the file and choose **Open With** followed by your browser.

If you downloaded the game ZIP, extract it first and open the `index.html` inside.

## Getting started

- Press **Space** to swing, or hold and release it to pitch. Use the **arrow keys** to aim and **P** or **Esc** to pause.
- Open **How to play** for the full control list, **Controls** to change your keys, or **Rules** in the Derby setup.
- Season, Pennant Cup, and Derby progress saves in your browser. Online and offline saves are separate.

## Report a bug

[Open a bug report on GitHub](https://github.com/jakefed1/Pennant-Planet/issues/new?template=bug-report.md). Include your browser, game mode, what happened, and the steps to reproduce it. If the bug happened during a game, include the ballpark and game situation. A screenshot or short clip helps.
