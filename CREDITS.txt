PENNANT PLANET — SOURCES AND ATTRIBUTION

ART AND INTERFACE
The packaged player portraits, club crests, character animations, stadium
drawings, and menu artwork were made for Pennant Planet. The night backdrop
is a render of Marcelo Stadium. Earlier character and team art was retired.

Season and Pennant Cup use the current roster art. Captains have signature special swing and pitch visuals.
Standard specials use a teal glow. Outfield chemistry uses a checkmark for good
chemistry, a cross for bad chemistry, and a dash for neutral. Three good
outfield pairs grant each outfielder +1 fielding.

VENUE REFERENCES
Real-world ballpark dimensions and visual references inform some venues.
The game draws the fields itself. Approximate layouts and fence heights may
differ from the real venues.

AUDIO
Gameplay effects and short cues are synthesized locally. The packaged crowd
sounds are edited, layered mixes of the recordings credited below.

All three sources below are dedicated to CC0 1.0:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

BACKGROUND: bmadore / Barry Madore — Crowd and field noise before and during
homerun hit at MLB baseball stadium. Target Field, Twins vs Red Sox, June 20,
2023. Only the pre-hit 0.4–11.6-second section is used; the subsequent bat,
home-run celebration and stadium music are excluded. Offset layers and light
filtering create a distant, denser crowd bed rather than a close conversation.
https://freesound.org/people/bmadore/sounds/691975/
Preview source: https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/691/691975_5111753-hq.mp3

SCORING / HOME-RUN ROAR: jasinski — yells.mp3, baseball game crowd cheering.
The 8.7–21.7-second section is blended with an offset section from 10.2–19.8
and a quiet layer of the stadium applause below.
https://freesound.org/people/jasinski/sounds/18365/
Preview source: https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/18/18365_71309-hq.mp3

ROUTINE APPLAUSE: cognito perceptu — applause.wav, applauding crowd in an
outdoor football stadium. The 0.15–5.65-second section supplies routine play
reactions and a little texture underneath big cheers.
https://freesound.org/people/cognito%20perceptu/sounds/57587/
Preview source: https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/57/57587_57789-hq.mp3
